照猫相机 · 帮助与支持

有问题、想要的姿势库里还没有，都可以发邮件给我们：copycatcam@blackbean.work。也可以在 App 里点 设置 → 意见反馈，会自动带上版本号。

怎么用

  1. 在取景框下方的姿势条里选一个姿势；往上拉可以按场景（街拍、咖啡店、海边……）或双人筛选。
  2. 姿势的剪影和线稿会叠在取景画面上。被拍的人挪到和轮廓重合，拿手机的人按快门就行。
  3. 右上角的按钮切换三种叠加方式：剪影+线稿、只要线稿、角落里的小参考图（小图可以拖到任意位置）。

常见问题

免费姿势什么时候换？

每天按手机的本地日期零点换一批新的免费姿势。锁着的姿势升级 Pro 后全部解锁。

买过 Pro，换了手机或重装后没了？

打开付费页，点底部的「恢复购买」。请确认登录的是当初购买用的 Apple ID。

有兑换码怎么用？

打开付费页，点底部的「兑换码」，输入即可。

怎么取消订阅？

iPhone 上打开 设置 → 点顶部你的名字 → 订阅 → 照猫相机，选择取消。你是 Pro 时，也可以在 App 的 设置 → 管理订阅 直接进入。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则会自动续订。退款请通过 reportaproblem.apple.com 向 Apple 申请。

照片存在哪里？

拍好的照片存在系统「照片」里的「Copycat Cam」相簿，App 里点左下角缩略图也能看到。照片只存在你的手机上，我们不会上传。

取景框是黑的 / 存不了照片？

打开 设置 → 照猫相机，确认「相机」和「照片」权限已打开。

怎么获取最新的姿势？

姿势库会自动更新；也可以在 设置 → 姿势库 → 检查更新 手动刷新。

Copycat Cam · Help & Support

Questions, problems, or a pose you'd like us to add? Email copycatcam@blackbean.work, or tap Settings → Send feedback in the app so your version number is included.

How it works

  1. Pick a pose from the strip below the viewfinder. Pull it up to filter by scene (Street, Café, Beach…) or duo poses.
  2. The pose's silhouette and outline appear over the live camera. The person in the photo moves until they match the shape, and whoever holds the phone taps the shutter.
  3. The button at the top right switches between three overlay styles: silhouette + outline, outline only, or a small reference photo in the corner that you can drag anywhere.

FAQ

When do the free poses change?

A new set of free poses unlocks every day at midnight, based on your phone's local date. Copycat Cam Pro unlocks every pose.

I bought Pro, but it's gone after reinstalling or switching phones.

Open the paywall and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I use a redemption code?

Open the paywall and tap "Redeem code" at the bottom.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Copycat Cam, then cancel. If you're a Pro member you can also get there from Settings → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my photos saved?

In the "Copycat Cam" album in the Photos app. Tap the thumbnail at the bottom left in the app to see them too. Photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

The viewfinder is black, or photos won't save.

Open Settings → Copycat Cam on your iPhone and make sure Camera and Photos access are turned on.

How do I get the newest poses?

The pose library updates on its own. You can also tap Settings → Pose library → Check for updates.
