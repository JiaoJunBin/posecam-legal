照猫相机隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 11 日

照猫相机（Copycat Cam，下称"本应用"）是一款拍照取景辅助工具：在相机取景画面上叠加参考姿势的剪影和线稿，帮助你摆姿势。我们尽量少收集数据，本政策说明本应用会处理哪些信息、为什么处理、以及你的选择。

一、我们不做的事

  • 本应用没有账号系统，不要求你注册或登录。
  • 相机画面、你拍的照片全部只在你的设备上处理和保存，不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 本应用不含广告，也不做跨应用追踪。
  • 我们不出售你的个人信息。

二、本应用处理的信息

1. 相机与照片

本应用需要相机权限来显示取景画面并拍照；需要相册权限把拍好的照片存进你相册里的「Copycat Cam」相簿，并在应用内显示这个相簿、导入你选择的照片。这些照片只存在你的设备上，我们收不到。

2. 运动传感器

本应用可能读取设备的运动状态，用来判断手机是否拿稳。该数据只在设备上使用，不保存、不上传。

3. 姿势库下载

本应用会从我们的服务器下载姿势库（清单、缩略图、剪影和线稿）。下载时服务器会看到你的 IP 地址和设备类型等常规网络信息，用于提供服务和统计访问量，日志保留不超过 30 天。

4. 设置与免费额度

你的设置（叠加方式、配色、语言等）、收藏的姿势和每天的免费姿势记录都保存在设备本地。

5. 购买（照猫相机 Pro）

订阅和买断通过 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 完成，我们看不到你的支付信息。我们使用 RevenueCat 处理购买凭证并确认你的 Pro 状态；RevenueCat 会收到匿名的应用用户 ID、购买凭证、设备类型和应用版本。RevenueCat 的隐私政策见 revenuecat.com/privacy。

6. 使用统计

为了了解功能怎么被使用、改进产品，本应用使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用数据：一个随机生成的应用实例 ID、你使用了哪些功能（例如选择了哪个姿势、打开了购买页面）、设备型号、系统版本、应用版本和大致的国家/地区。这些数据不与你的姓名、邮箱等身份信息关联，也不用于广告。Google 的隐私政策见 policies.google.com/privacy。

7. 崩溃与诊断

为了修复问题，本应用会收集匿名的崩溃报告和性能诊断信息（崩溃堆栈、设备型号、系统版本、应用版本），不包含你的照片或画面内容。中国大陆以外由 Google Firebase Crashlytics 和 Performance Monitoring 处理，中国大陆由 Sentry 处理（隐私政策见 sentry.io/privacy）。

三、数据的保留与删除

存在设备上的数据随卸载应用一起删除。购买记录由 Apple/Google 和 RevenueCat 按其政策保留。你可以通过下方联系方式要求我们删除与你的匿名应用用户 ID 关联的记录。

四、儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下（或你所在地区法定年龄以下）的儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

五、数据跨境

姿势库服务器、RevenueCat、Google Firebase 和 Sentry 的服务器可能位于你所在国家/地区之外。我们会按适用法律采取保护措施。

六、你的权利

依据你所在地区的法律（如 GDPR、CCPA），你可能有权访问、更正、删除你的个人信息，或反对、限制某些处理。你可以通过下方联系方式行使这些权利，也可以随时在系统设置中撤回相机和相册权限。

七、政策变更

本政策更新时会在此页面发布并更新生效日期，重大变更会在应用内提示。

八、联系我们

邮箱：copycatcam@blackbean.work

Copycat Cam Privacy Policy

Effective: September 11, 2026

Copycat Cam (the "App") is a camera assistant that overlays reference pose silhouettes and line art on your viewfinder to help you pose. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the App processes, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we do not do

  • The App has no accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • Your camera feed and the photos you take are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • The App has no ads and does not track you across other apps.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information the App processes

Camera and photos

The App needs camera access to show the viewfinder and take photos, and photo library access to save them to a "Copycat Cam" album, show that album inside the App, and import photos you choose. These photos stay on your device and we never receive them.

Motion sensors

The App may read device motion to tell whether the phone is held steady. This is used on-device only and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Pose library downloads

The App downloads the pose library (manifest, thumbnails, silhouettes and line art) from our servers. As with any download, our servers see standard network information such as your IP address and device type, used to serve the content and count traffic. Logs are kept for no more than 30 days.

Settings, favorites and free poses

Your settings (overlay mode, look, language and so on), the poses you favorite, and which free poses you saw today are stored locally on your device.

Purchases (Copycat Cam Pro)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchase receipt, device type and app version. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Usage analytics

To understand how features are used and improve the App, we use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data: a randomly generated app instance ID, which features you use (for example which pose you picked, or that the purchase screen was opened), device model, OS version, app version and approximate country. This data is not linked to your name, email or other identity, and is not used for advertising. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Crash and diagnostics

To fix problems, the App collects anonymous crash reports and performance diagnostics (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version). These never include your photos or camera content. Outside mainland China they are processed by Google Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring; in mainland China by Sentry (see sentry.io/privacy).

3. Retention and deletion

On-device data is removed when you delete the App. Purchase records are retained by Apple/Google and RevenueCat under their own policies. You can ask us to delete records tied to your anonymous app user ID using the contact below.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. International transfers

Our library servers, RevenueCat, Google Firebase and Sentry may operate outside your country. We apply safeguards required by applicable law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us below to exercise these rights. You can withdraw camera and photo permissions in your system settings at any time.

7. Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes are announced in the App.
